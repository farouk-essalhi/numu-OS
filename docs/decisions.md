# Decisions

One line per decision and why. Newest at the bottom. "Proposed" = drafted in Phase 0, needs Ali and Farouk's sign-off; change the tag to "Agreed" (or rewrite the line) at the workshop.

| # | Date | Status | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Money is `bigint` minor units in `*_minor` columns with a `currency` column (default QAR), not "fils" columns | Same rule as the plan (QAR × 100, no decimals), but leaves room for a client billed in USD |
| 2 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Dates are Doha dates; SQL uses `private.qatar_today()`; week is Sun–Sat, working days Sun–Thu | Due dates and "this week" must match how the team works, regardless of server time zone |
| 3 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Roles come from `team_members.access_level` and `contacts.user_id`; no separate profiles table | Keeps to the planned tables; a person has one row that says what they can do |
| 4 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Invite-only login: signups off, logins linked to people by email automatically | Nobody outside NUMU and its clients can create an account; adding a person and inviting them is one step |
| 5 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Retainers and projects (fees) are visible to partners and account leads only; creators see scope, not prices | Client pricing shouldn't leak to the whole team; account leads run the client relationship |
| 6 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Clients get no direct table access; the phase 3 portal uses narrow functions that hide internal comments and revision counts | RLS can hide rows but not columns; a function boundary is the safe way to give clients a partial view |
| 7 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Deliverable types are a lookup table with effort points, not an enum | Points will be tuned; changing a row is easier than changing a type |
| 8 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | The monthly generator lives in Postgres and runs from pg_cron on the 25th, 09:00 Doha | Runs even if the app is down; the same function serves the app, cron and (later) Claude via MCP |
| 9 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Generator is idempotent (unique scope line + month + seq); raising a quantity adds the difference, lowering never deletes | Safe to re-run by hand; deleting planned work automatically would surprise people |
| 10 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Generated due dates are spread evenly over the month's working days; post dates stay empty until scheduling | Gives a sensible default plan for Sunday planning; real post dates come from the content calendar |
| 11 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | `due_date` = ready for Client review; `post_date` = go-live; overdue = past due and still with the team, or approved but not posted on time | Separates the team's deadline from the client's publishing date, so late work and slow approvals aren't mixed up |
| 12 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Status history is written only by a trigger; revision count only by the trigger (Client review → earlier status = +1) | The on-time and turnaround metrics are only worth something if nobody can edit the history |
| 13 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Only partners and account leads move work into/out of Client review or into Approved; any other move is free, steps can be skipped | Protects sign-off without making daily card moves slower than WhatsApp |
| 14 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Every deliverable belongs to exactly one retainer or project; a retainer item without a scope line is an "extra outside scope" | Lets us see over-servicing per client, which feeds margin |
| 15 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | A retainer starting mid-month still gets the full monthly quantity; the account lead trims it | Pro-rating rules vary per contract; a person decides faster than a rule |
| 16 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Price changes create a new retainer row; quantity changes edit the scope line | Keeps invoice history true without versioning every scope line |
| 17 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Posted does not require a post link in the database (the app asks for one) | Stories and some formats have no permanent link; a hard rule would block real work |
| 18 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Only Phase 1 tables are migrated now; later tables are documented in `docs/schema.md` and migrated in their phase | "Build only what we use weekly"; schemas are cheaper to change before they exist |
| 19 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Database tests are pgTAP, runnable on the Supabase stack (`supabase test db`) or plain Postgres (`scripts/db/test.sh`) | Access rules are the biggest risk (client/finance leaks), so they are tested automatically, not only by hand at each gate |
| 20 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | The UI follows the reference screens Farouk shared; their "Messages" tab becomes the Board and "Performance" becomes Pulse (delivery %, approval time, capacity in points) | Chat and minute-by-minute time tracking are out of scope; the layout still fits the work |
| 21 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Until the Supabase project exists, the app runs on demo data in the browser, shaped exactly like the database; `lib/store.tsx` is the only file that changes to connect it | Lets the workshop react to real screens now without building throwaway backend code |
| 22 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Screens render in the browser per person and per day (`use(browser())` behind a static skeleton) | "Today", "due in 2 days" and the Doha greeting can't be baked in at build time |
| 23 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | On Friday and Saturday, "this week" means the coming Sunday–Saturday; Board and Schedule open there too | Qatar's weekend: on Saturday the team is planning the week ahead, not reviewing a finished one |
| 24 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | The workflow rules are mirrored in `lib/workflow.ts` so the app only offers moves that will succeed; the database stays the authority, and a test checks the due-date spread against its output | Fewer dead-end taps for the team without trusting the client for security |
| 25 | 2026-10-31 | Proposed | Hand-built UI kit + vaul for sheets; shadcn/ui components are added only when a screen needs one | The reference look is custom; fewer generated components to maintain |
| 26 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | The UI is rebuilt as a responsive product (sidebar, dense dashboards, tables, board/list) adapted from Farouk's second set of references; this replaces the phone-only look from decision 20 | The first version felt like a one-off phone screen, not the platform partners plan from on a laptop and the team uses on a phone |
| 27 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Navigation: Overview, My work, Approvals, Deliverables, Calendar, Clients, Campaigns, Capacity, Reports; Approvals is its own page with a badge | Sign-off and client waits are the core of NUMU's loop and the most time-sensitive work, so they get a first-class queue |
| 28 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Client health: Behind = 2+ late items; At risk = 1 late item or a client wait over 48h; otherwise On track | Simple enough to trust at a glance; uses flags the database already computes |
| 29 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Charts are hand-built (no chart library yet) and follow the data-viz rules: fixed categorical order, reserved status colours with icon + label, legends, tooltips, hidden tables | Small, fast, accessible; Recharts can come in with Phase 5 reports if needed |
| 30 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Interim sign-in until Supabase: email + password per team member on dummy @numu.test addresses, checked on the server against salted scrypt hashes in the Netlify env var NUMU_ACCOUNTS, with a signed 30-day cookie (NUMU_SESSION_SECRET); every page is behind it. Off when those env vars are missing (local dev) | Farouk asked for logins now; the site was public; no passwords or hashes ever go in the (public) repo. Supabase auth replaces it later, and everyone gets a new password then |
| 31 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | The demo team uses the real team's first names (Farouk, Ali, Rand, Khaled, Anas, Ruba, Busafen); Rand is the account lead and the other four are creators until told otherwise; clients and work stay fictional | Signing in as yourself should show your own view; roles are a placeholder for the Phase 0 workshop |
| 32 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Supabase project `numu-os` in Mumbai (ap-south-1), free tier; migrations applied, no seed; the seven team members added | Mumbai is the closest Supabase region to Doha; the real project starts empty so NUMU enters its own clients |
| 33 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Sign-in is Supabase Auth with email + password (users created by a partner in the Supabase dashboard, signups off), replacing decision 30's interim logins; magic links come back when people sign in with real email addresses | The team's @numu.test addresses can't receive email; passwords live only in Supabase, never in the repo or Netlify |
| 34 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | The app talks to Supabase from the browser only (publishable key + the person's session, all access through RLS); the session guard is in the browser (`components/shell/app-root.tsx`), pages are static shells with no data | No server code holds data or keys, so there is nothing to leak server-side and no `proxy.ts` (decision 30's Netlify problem). | 35 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Without the two `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_*` env vars the app runs on demo data (local development, tests), with "View as" and "Start empty" in Settings | Screens can be worked on and tested without touching real data |
| 36 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Data entry in the app: clients, contacts, retainers, scope lines and campaigns (leads and partners), team members (partners), deleting deliverables (leads and partners), and "Generate {month}" on a client calls `generate_month_deliverables` for that retainer | NUMU enters its own data; generating by hand covers clients signed mid-month without waiting for the 25th |
| 37 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | The board is drag and drop: by status (same sign-off rules as the database, refused drops say why) and by person (reassigns); press-and-hold on phones; cards snap into the slot where they will land | Moving work should be faster than a WhatsApp message; the rules stay in the database |
| 38 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Creators see a client's scope through its deliverables (they can't read retainers, which hold fees) | Keeps fees out of creators' reach without a new access rule; a brand-new retainer's scope shows to creators once its month is generated |
| 39 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Tasks that aren't content (client follow-ups, reports, content calendars, meetings, other) are rows in `deliverables` with a non-content type (`deliverable_types.is_content = false`): Planned or Done only (Done is a new, tasks-only status), a client but no retainer or campaign required, never an "extra", left out of content metrics. The app says "task" for everything and "deliverable" for content | One board, My work, calendar and capacity for all work, without diluting on-time and approval numbers; agencies spend real time on non-content work |
| 40 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Recurring tasks are scope lines; a scope line with quantity 1 can pin `due_day` (next working day if it falls on a weekend, last working day if the month is shorter). "Repeat every month" on New task adds that scope line | Reuses the monthly generator instead of a second recurrence system; reports and calendars have fixed days, unlike content |
| 41 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Generated titles drop "1/1" when there is one a month ("Report", "Long video · YouTube") | Reads naturally for recurring tasks and single items |
| 42 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Creators still can't add tasks (leads and partners plan), but anyone on the team can tick a task done | Same planning rule as deliverables; revisit if creators need their own to-dos |
| 43 | 2026-11-04 | Proposed | Workflow permissions are per person (`team_members.can_approve`, `can_send_to_client`, `can_create_tasks`), switched by partners in Team → Permissions. Defaults: only partners approve; partners and account leads send to clients and record answers; everyone creates and assigns tasks. Replaces decision 13's sign-off rule and decision 42's "creators can't add tasks" | Farouk: only he and Ali say content is good to go, and the whole team adds and assigns tasks.
